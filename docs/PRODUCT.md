# ANY — Product Brief

## Positioning

ANY is an independent AI voice dictation platform that converts natural speech into accurate, readable text and makes that workflow available across devices.

## Primary users

1. Professionals who write messages, email, notes and documents all day.
2. Founders and operators who capture thoughts while moving between tasks.
3. People who prefer speaking to typing or need an accessible input method.
4. Teams that want consistent terminology and writing style.

## Core user journey

1. Discover ANY through the marketing site.
2. Compare Free, Pro and Team plans.
3. Create an account and complete onboarding.
4. Grant microphone permission with clear purpose and consent.
5. Record speech and receive a transcript.
6. Apply formatting preferences and personal vocabulary.
7. Insert, copy or save the result.
8. Monitor usage and manage subscription settings.

## MVP acceptance criteria

- Responsive marketing and pricing pages.
- Authentication with verified email and session handling.
- Server-side plan entitlements and usage accounting.
- A working transcription flow with provider abstraction.
- Clear microphone permission and recording state.
- User-controlled history retention and deletion.
- Stripe checkout and billing portal after webhook verification is implemented.
- Tests for tenant isolation, authorization, billing state and usage limits.

## Commercial model

Free is an acquisition and evaluation tier. Pro is an individual subscription. Team is a per-seat subscription with centralized billing and shared workspace controls. Pricing in the first UI is illustrative and must not be treated as a live offer until billing, taxes, quotas and terms are configured.
